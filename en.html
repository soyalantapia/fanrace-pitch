The market gap

Four groups.
Zero connection between them.

Amateur motorsport runs fragmented and blind: every actor on its own tool, with no memory between events.

Organizers

Sportity · Excel

The whole operation by hand, with no single source of truth for the event.

Competitors

Forms · paper

The same data re entered at every event, with errors each time.

Staff

WhatsApp · radio

Scattered comms, no traceability or real time position.

Fans

Facebook · scrapers

They live outside the platform. No identity or data returns.

Nobody shares identity, data or economy.

The stakes

Lives are on the line.
In every line of code.

At the Rally de Córdoba a spectator died, standing on the wrong side of a curve. Between one checkpoint and the next, the organization has no way to know where people are. It is a blind hour, every stage.

The product's goal
Race direction sees the position of marshals and fans, and can act before something happens.
Protects lives.

That is why we treat it as critical software, not just another form.

The insight

The identity layer IS the product.

One account accrues roles: today a fan, tomorrow a competitor, next year a marshal. Roles are relationships, not attributes: they add up, never replace, and data is entered once.

fanrace.app · /organize
Marshal PWA · for officials on track · mobile

Works
with no signal.

Offline app, for one hand, in the middle of the stage.

  • Pass logging and reports, even with no connection.
  • Every pass is sealed: no edits, audit grade.
  • Syncs when signal returns: every pass persisted and reordered by timestamp and sequence, with deterministic conflict resolution.
Race Control · for race direction · desktop

The surface that sees
it all in time.

The real time board that was missing in Córdoba.

  • Position of marshals and incidents live, in under 5 seconds with signal; with no signal, last known position that syncs on reconnect.
  • Targeted Red Flag: a directed alert to the exact sector, with press and hold to confirm.
  • Full audit trail and signed PDF for safety and regulation.

We work fan positioning for safety, never car tracking: we respect the boundary with Spectra.
